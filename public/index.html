How it works

  1. Choose a package

    Pick the transformation that fits, then check out securely with Stripe.

  2. Send the drawing

    Right after checkout you'll land on an upload page: send a clear photo of the drawing, pick a style, and tell us the story behind it. (If that page doesn't appear, reply to your Stripe receipt email with the photo instead.) A parent or guardian confirms consent.

  3. Review your proof

    We make the art — AI-assisted and hand-reviewed — and within 5 business days you get a private, watermarked proof. Ask for changes if your package includes revisions.

  4. Get the final art

    Approve the proof and we deliver your digital files. Order a canvas or mug keepsake afterward if you like.

Questions

How do I send the drawing?

Right after checkout you'll land on an upload page where you send a clear photo of the drawing and a sentence or two about what it shows. If that page doesn't appear, reply to your Stripe receipt email with the photo instead. You can also use the contact page.

What kind of photo should I send?

A clear phone photo works best: lay the drawing flat in good light and fill the frame. JPG, PNG, WEBP or HEIC, up to 15 MB.

How is the art made?

Every piece starts with your child's drawing. We use AI tools to transform it, and a real artist reviews every piece before it becomes your proof — AI-assisted and hand-reviewed.

When should I order for Christmas?

Order by December 5 for Christmas delivery. Your proof arrives within 5 business days of us receiving the drawing, so earlier is better.

How long does it take?

Your private proof is ready within 5 business days of receiving the drawing.

What if we want changes?

Magic Reveal includes 1 revision round and the Big Imagination Bundle includes up to 2. Send focused feedback when you review the proof.

Can we use the art for a business?

Packages are for your family's personal enjoyment. Ask us first before using the artwork for a business, promotion or other commercial purpose.
