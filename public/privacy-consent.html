Privacy and guardian consent

Private by default

Orders, drawings, stories, proofs and finished artwork are handled privately. We use them only to make and deliver your order.

Guardian authorization

A parent or legal guardian must authorize sending a child's drawing to WonderDraw. We confirm this when you send the drawing after checkout.

Sharing is a separate choice

We never post a child's artwork, story or reaction video in a gallery or on social media unless the child's parent or legal guardian separately opts in. A first name, a nickname or no name at all is fine.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We don't see or store your full card number.

Deletion and privacy requests

To ask a privacy question or request deletion, use the contact page with your name and order reference if you have one. Please don't send sensitive information in the message.

This page explains how we handle your information; it isn't legal advice. Families with specific requests are welcome to contact us.
