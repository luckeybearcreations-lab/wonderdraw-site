Guides

A gift made from your child’s drawing

Grandparents already have the mug with a stock photo on it. What they do not have is the dragon your five-year-old drew, finished as real art. Here is what you can make from one drawing, and when to order.

Start with the drawing they are proudest of

Pick one with a story behind it: a pet, the family, a made-up creature with a name. Drawings with a clear main character work best. Our guide to photographing a drawing covers how to get a good photo with a phone.

Three ways to give it

Finished digital art. Spark Sketch ($24) turns one drawing into one piece of finished art in the style you choose, delivered as digital files. Print it at home or at a photo counter, or send it to family far away.

Art plus a reveal video. Magic Reveal ($49) adds a short before-and-after video that moves from the original drawing to the finished art, with one revision round. It is the one to pick if the gift will be opened on a phone or a video call.

A full scene. The Big Imagination Bundle ($79) builds a custom background scene around the drawing, with an extended reveal video, up to two revision rounds and multiple file formats.

Something to hold

After you approve your proof, the finished art can go on a canvas ($39) or an 11oz mug ($24). Keepsakes are made to order, and we confirm availability, final price and shipping with you before anything is ordered. If you need one by a certain date, tell us when you ask.

When to order for Christmas

Order by December 5 for Christmas delivery of the digital art. Your private proof arrives within 5 business days of us receiving the drawing, and a revision takes more time, so earlier is better.

What the child sees

Their own drawing, still recognizably theirs, with the same characters and the same odd colors. The art is AI-assisted and hand-reviewed, and you approve a private proof before anything is final.

Privacy

Orders, drawings and proofs stay private. A first name, a nickname or no name at all is fine, and nothing is shared publicly without a guardian’s separate permission. Details are on the privacy and guardian consent page.
