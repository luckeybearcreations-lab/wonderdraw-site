Terms and order expectations

How an order works

After checkout, a parent or guardian sends the drawing and its story. We create the transformation and send a private, watermarked proof within 5 business days, then deliver the final digital files once you approve.

Proofs and revisions

Revisions depend on your package: Magic Reveal includes 1 revision round and the Big Imagination Bundle includes up to 2. Please share focused feedback during the proof stage.

Little Artist Club

The club renews monthly until you cancel. Cancel anytime from the link in your Stripe receipt or by contacting us; it stops before the next billing date.

Personal use

Finished artwork is for your family's personal enjoyment. Ask before using it for a business, promotion or other commercial purpose.

Physical keepsakes

Keepsakes are requested after proof approval. We confirm availability, price and shipping before placing any physical order.

Refunds

If we haven't started your artwork yet, contact us for a full refund. Once a proof has been delivered, we'll work with you through your included revisions to get it right.

Support

Questions about an order or which package to choose? Visit the contact page.
