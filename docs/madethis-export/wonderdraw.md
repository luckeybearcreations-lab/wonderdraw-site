- Headline: "Your child imagined it. We bring it to life."
- Pitch: turns children's drawings into artist-reviewed digital art and before-and-after reveal videos. Guardians choose a package, upload the drawing and its story after checkout, receive a private watermarked proof, then digital delivery.

- Trust line: proofs ready within 5 business days; private by default.

## Packages (comparison table on the site)
- **Spark Sketch ($24)**: one drawing, one art style, artist-reviewed digital transformation, digital files. No reveal video.
- **Magic Reveal ($49, "recommended")**: digital transformation, short reveal video, protected proof + one revision, digital final.
- **Big Imagination Bundle ($79, inferred)**: custom background scene, extended reveal video, protected proof, up to 2 revision rounds, multiple digital formats.
- **Little Artist Club ($12/month)**: "Prefer a monthly digital transformation?" Recurring digital transformations.

## Process
1. Choose a package and check out securely.
2. Guardian uploads the drawing and shares its story; confirms guardian consent.
3. Artist review and creation.
4. Private, watermarked proof within 5 business days; revisions per package.
5. Approve; digital delivery. Physical keepsakes optional, quoted after approval.

## Keepsakes page (/keepsakes)
- For families who already approved their digital proof.
- Canvas keepsake ($39) and 11oz mug keepsake ($24), made to order; availability, exact price and shipping confirmed before any order.
- Private by default. Quote form fields: name, email, digital order or contact reference, keepsake choice, approved-artwork reference, optional shipping location (no full street address). Submitting requests a quote, not an order.

## Privacy & guardian consent (/privacy-consent)
- Orders, drawings, stories, proofs and finished artwork are private by default.
- "A parent or legal guardian must authorize submission of a child's drawing" during post-checkout intake.
- Gallery features, social posts or reaction-video shares need a separate guardian opt-in.
- Deletion/privacy requests via contact, with name and order reference; no sensitive info in the message.
- Notes it isn't legal advice.

## Terms / order expectations (/terms)
- Workflow: checkout → guardian uploads drawing and story → transformation → private watermarked proof → final digital delivery.

- Personal use only; "Ask before using artwork for a business, promotion, or other commercial purpose."
- Keepsakes requested after proof approval; availability, price and shipping confirmed first.
- Support via contact page.
